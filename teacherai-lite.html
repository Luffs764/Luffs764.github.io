      What works in this page
      • Lessons: AI writes the lesson from a topic you type, or only from the notes you paste. Inside Claude it uses your own Claude usage and asks your permission first.
      • Without AI (for example after a subscription ends): the built-in engine builds the lesson from pasted notes, with no account or server. In the saved copy you can add your own Claude or Grok key.
      • Voice: Claude's voices where Claude offers them (a computer browser or the Claude desktop app). Otherwise the best voice on your device: on iPhone, download a free Premium voice such as Ava or Zoe in Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices. In the saved copy opened in a browser, natural Kokoro voices (Heart, Michael, Bella, Fenrir) also run on your own device after a one-time download of about 95 MB. Use Test voice if you hear nothing.
      • Save app copy keeps this page as one file that runs in any browser.
      • Only in the full TeacherAI app: MP4 video export, PDF and photo reading, podcasts.
